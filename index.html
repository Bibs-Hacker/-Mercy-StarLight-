Chapter 02 • A letter from me to you

Dear Mercy...

My love,

I don't even know where to begin because whenever I try to put everything I feel for you into words, the words suddenly become too small.

But I'll try anyway.

Mercy, I want you to know that I appreciate you. Not just the obvious things. Not just your smile, your voice, your presence or the beautiful moments we share.

I appreciate the little things. The random conversations. The jokes that make absolutely no sense. The moments when we're both being stubborn. The moments when we're laughing like children. The moments when one of us says something completely ridiculous and somehow the other person still finds it funny. 😂❤️

I love those moments because they are ours.

And if life ever becomes difficult, I hope you remember that you don't have to face everything alone.

I want to be the person who reminds you that you are capable when you forget. The person who tells you to keep going when you're tired. The person who celebrates you when things go right. And the person who stays close when things don't.

I don't promise that life will always be perfect.

Actually... let's be realistic. We are probably going to annoy each other approximately seventeen thousand times. 😂

There will be misunderstandings. There will be stressful days. There will be moments when one of us needs space and moments when the other needs reassurance.

But my promise is not perfection.

My promise is that I will keep choosing communication, honesty, patience, forgiveness and love.

I want us to grow. Not just grow older. Grow wiser. Grow stronger. Grow financially. Grow emotionally. Grow spiritually. Grow professionally. Grow into two people who can look back someday and say:

“Wow... we really built something beautiful.”

And Mercy, please never underestimate yourself.

There are days when you might feel behind. There are days when you might compare yourself to somebody else. There are days when your dreams may seem too far away.

On those days, I want you to remember this:

You are still becoming.

You don't have to have everything figured out today.

Keep learning. Keep trying. Keep dreaming. Keep praying. Keep working. Keep becoming the woman you want to be.

And whenever you win, even if the victory seems tiny, I want to celebrate it with you.

If you pass an exam: I'm celebrating.

If you accomplish a difficult task: I'm celebrating.

If you finally achieve something you've been working toward for months: I'm probably going to celebrate louder than you. 😂❤️

And if you fail?

Then we learn.

If you fall?

Then we breathe.

If you're tired?

Then we rest.

But we don't give up on the beautiful future we're building.

Because I don't want a love that exists only when everything is easy.

I want a love that knows how to survive the storms.

A love that can laugh in the middle of chaos.

A love that can say “I'm sorry.”

A love that can say “I understand.”

A love that can say “Let's try again.”

And sometimes... a love that can simply say:

“Come here.” ❤️

Now let's talk about something extremely serious.

If a ghost ever appears in our house, you are officially responsible. 👻😂

Because apparently loving you has already opened a portal to another dimension.

Somewhere out there is probably a ghost watching us and saying:

“These two are doing too much.” 👻💀😂

But honestly?

Let them watch.

Because I would rather be remembered as the guy who loved you too loudly than the guy who loved you quietly because he was afraid to show it.

I want you to know that your happiness matters to me.

Your dreams matter. Your fears matter. Your goals matter. Your little victories matter. Your bad days matter.

You matter.

And I hope that whenever you doubt that, you come back to this little universe and remember that somewhere there is a man who sat down and decided:

“I'm going to build something just for her.”

Not because a website can measure love.

It can't.

But because sometimes love needs somewhere to leave fingerprints.

This is one of mine.

So here is my promise:

I will keep learning how to love you better.

I will keep supporting your dreams.

I will celebrate your growth.

I will laugh with you.

I will annoy you occasionally. Okay... maybe frequently. 😂

I will remind you that you're beautiful even on the days you don't feel beautiful.

I will remind you that you're strong when you feel weak.

And I will remind you that your story isn't finished just because today's chapter is difficult.

There are still pages we haven't written.

Places we haven't visited.

Dreams we haven't achieved.

Photos we haven't taken.

Arguments we haven't had. 😂

Jokes we haven't laughed at.

Adventures we haven't experienced.

And countless ordinary days that might secretly become our favorite memories.

So, Mercy...

Let's not rush our story.

Let's live it.

Let's build it.

Let's protect it.

Let's laugh through it.

Let's grow through it.

And most importantly...

Let's keep choosing each other.

Through ordinary mornings. Through crazy nights. Through beautiful surprises. Through scary seasons. Through every little haunted adventure. 👻❤️

Because if I get to walk through life with someone who can make me smile, challenge me, inspire me, annoy me, make me laugh and still somehow make my heart feel at home...

then I already have something incredibly special.

And that something is you.

Forever grateful for you.

With all my heart,
Brian ❤️
